# Arrow pitch (SpeedHacks)

Three slides, then the live demo.

| | |
|---|---|
| ![Slide 1](slide-1.png) | **1. Arrow points you to your…** cofounder, investor, next hire, future wife (cycles live in `index.html`) |
| ![Slide 2](slide-2.png) | **2. Call. Match. Meet.** Pick up the phone, we match you, tap your text link → live demo |
| ![Slide 3](slide-3.png) | **3. 9 years of small talk, saved at SF Tech Week.** + the judging criteria |

- **Present:** open `index.html` in a browser (full screen), or use `arrow-pitch.pdf`.
- **The math on slide 3:** 1,593 events is the official count on tech-week.com/calendar/sf for Oct 5–11, 2026. 50 guests per event is our assumption. 1 hour per guest is the time to find the right person by walking the room (about 10 conversations). 1,593 × 50 × 1 h ≈ 80,000 h ≈ 9.1 years. Also: 65 Tech Week events were tagged "Matchmaking".
